### Wave 3: Flight info and route checks

Shared: `backend/data/navaids.py`, `ui/modals/flight_info.py`, the ETA path in `backend/core/`. Review: `code-review` with an aviation read of each verdict. Verification: each item first reproduces on fixture or live data; a confirmed defect gets a red test before its fix.

- [ ] 854 AWY1 segment records carry a different MEA for the opposite direction (V1 sequence 160: 2,100 ft one way, 5,000 the other); `get_max_mea_for_route` always reads the first value. Read the direction of travel. After the airway-walk fix: `_stretch_segments` (`backend/data/navaids.py`) uses the main MEA and `_AirwayStretch` drops the direction (it keeps min/max indices); keep the direction and pick the MEA whose "xx BND" text matches the segment's course, or conservatively `max(mea, mea_opposite)`.
- [ ] Adjacent airways with no fix between them (`ABC J1 J2 DEF`) use ABC and DEF as both airways' endpoints, so the MEA check shows "MEA not checked: J2 (ABC not on it)" naming the wrong fix (`backend/data/navaids.py` `_find_airway_stretch`). Use the one point both airways share as the junction; with none or several, log only. No such pair in the 1,914 IFR routes of `data/test-vatsim-data.json`.
- [ ] Non-US filed routes resolve idents against US NASR only (`navaids.py:416`, `:446`, `:1039`): AIB256's `CAN` resolves to 47.41N 122.84W and its `TNT T420 WELIN` splices in US T420; a garbled route longer than the great circle is accepted (`calculations.py:130`, 10,992 nm vs 4,900). Skip route distance when the departure or arrival is outside NASR coverage, or bound route over great circle. Measurements: `.tmp/map-nonus-routes.md` (WIA357: SKB resolves to a Texas VOR, ETA 30.33 h against 0.346 h).

  Settled (owner): ETA uses great circle when either end is outside the US, and also when a route is over 2× great circle and more than 100 nm longer. "In the US" is an ICAO prefix test: K, PA, PH, PG, PW, PM, TJ, TI, NS. `parse_route_string` stays as it is (route weather and the MEA check use it); only the ETA use is gated.
