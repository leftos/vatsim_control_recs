Where the Name, Add search and Members sit

A1

Two panes side by side

Name on top, Add search on the left, Members on the right, all visible at once; Tab moves Name → Add → Members.

Needs about 110 columns. In an 80-column terminal the modal shrinks to 80 and both panes squeeze to about 35 columns, so names and the Members hint truncate.

A2

One list, Tab switches Members and Add

One full-width list under the Name field; Tab flips it between the member list and the Add search with its results.

96 columns and fits 80 with room to spare, but you see either the members or the search results, never both.

A3

Members on top, Add search with a dropdown below

The member list is always shown; the Add search sits under it and its results open as a dropdown beneath the box.

One column, 96 wide and fits 80. The modal grows by the dropdown's height while results are open, and Esc closes the dropdown before it cancels.

How an untracked airport shows in Members and in Add results

Same list in each lane. The Members cursor (unfocused) is on KOAK; the Add cursor is on KMCC, which is not tracked, to show how each mark reads under the highlight.

B1

Dim "not tracked" suffix

The words "not tracked" in dim text after the airport name; nothing to learn.

Takes 13 columns on those rows, and at narrow widths the name truncates first to keep it.

B2

○ glyph before the ICAO, legend in the hint

A ○ in its own column before the ICAO; the hint line ends with "○ not tracked".

Costs 2 columns on every row and keeps names whole, but the meaning lives in the legend.

B3

Whole row dimmed, no text

The untracked row is drawn in dim text, filter included, with no word or glyph.

Costs no columns, but nothing says what dim means, it looks like the dim section headers, and under the cursor it fades to a lighter blue.
